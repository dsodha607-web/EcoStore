Daily Green Habit

Simple Tips to Reduce Plastic Today

  • 1 Swap plastic toothbrushes for compostable organic bamboo brushes every 3 months.
  • 2 Replace cling wrap with washable beeswax food wraps to keep produce fresh naturally.
  • 3 Carry a stainless steel water bottle & organic cotton tote bag wherever you go.
